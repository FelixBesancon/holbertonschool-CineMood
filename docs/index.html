Features

A full-stack web app for film lovers

CinéMood is a single-page web application developed in Python with FastAPI and built using React and Tailwind CSS.
It combines a personal movie library with an AI-powered recommendation engine, all filtered based on the streaming platforms that you're actually subscribed to.

Film Library

Search for any film in the TMDB catalog, build a watchlist of your favorites, and log your viewing history with a tier list ranking and emotional tags designed to reflect your mood while watching.

AI Recommendations

Answer a quick mood questionnaire, swipe through candidates, and get personalized movie suggestions selected to match your current mood, along with with a match score from Mistral AI.

Mobile-First & Responsive

Designed from the ground up for mobile devices, CinéMood offers a seamless browsing experience that adapts to all screen sizes.

AI Recommendations

From mood to film
in three guided steps

CinéMood uses Mistral AI to turn your current state of mind
into a personalized selection of movies, tailored to your platforms
and based on your watchlist and viewing history.

Step 01

Answer the questionnaire

A short questionnaire with 5 multiple-choice questions helps the tool assess your mood: your energy level, the context of your session, the type of experience you're looking for etc..
You can add an optional note with free-form text for more specific requests.
No need to spend time writing a complex request-just one minute is all it takes to describe exactly what you want!

Step 02

Swipe the deck

Mistral AI generates a deck of candidate film cards based on your profile and the answers you provided in the previous step.
Just like a dating app, swipe right to indicate you like the suggestion, and left to reject it.
Your preferences are gathered locally and sent in a single call at the end - so there's no delay between swipes.

Step 03

Get your results

Finally, a second AI call processes your likes, dislikes, history, and watchlist together, and you receive a list of movie suggestions that are 100% personalized for you and tailored to your current mood.
Each suggestion comes with a match score out of 100, and a personalized explanation of why this film fits your mood and desired experience.
From there, you can save the movies you want and add them to your watchlist.

About

The story behind it

"What do I actually feel like watching tonight?"

As a lifelong movie enthusiast myself, I decided to create CinéMood because I was tired of the same old evening routine: opening a streaming app, scrolling through titles for 45 minutes, and eventually giving up. The problem wasn't the catalog itself, but the fact that there was no tool that could provide varied, personalized recommendations in a simple and intuitive way.

The project started as a simple film logging app. Over six weeks and six sprints, it grew into a full AI-powered recommendation engine, with a PostgreSQL database, a FastAPI backend following a strict layered architecture, and a React frontend designed for mobile from the start.

Developing it on my own forced me to make every decision: from designing the data model to engineering the prompts, from JWT authentication to deployment on Vercel. It was up to me to diagnose every bug and every pattern mine to choose..

CinéMood is my end-of-year portfolio project at Holberton School Bordeaux, where I'm studying application design and development. The entire codebase is available as open source on GitHub.
