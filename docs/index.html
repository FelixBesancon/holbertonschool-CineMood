Features

Everything about
how you watch films

01

AI Recommendation Flow

Answer a short mood questionnaire, then swipe through a curated deck of candidates. CinéMood uses Mistral AI to match your profile — viewing history, streaming platforms, and tonight's mood — and returns personalized picks with a match score out of 100.

02

Personal Film Diary

Log every film you watch. Rate it with a six-tier Prestige system — from Platinum to Trash — and tag it with one of 50 emotional markers like "Hidden Gem", "Emotional Damage", or "So Stupid It's Good". Your history shapes future recommendations.

03

Platform-Aware Suggestions

CinéMood only recommends films you can actually watch tonight. Select your active streaming platforms once, and every suggestion — including picks from your own watchlist — is filtered accordingly. No more "available on 4 services you don't have".

About

The story behind it

I built CinéMood because I was tired of the same Friday evening ritual: open a streaming app, scroll for 45 minutes, give up. The problem wasn't the catalogue — it was that no tool asked the right question first. What do I actually feel like watching tonight?

The project started as a simple logging app and grew into a full AI-powered recommendation engine over six weeks and six sprints. I designed the data model, built the backend API from scratch in FastAPI, integrated Mistral AI with a custom two-step prompt system, and shipped a responsive React frontend — solo, from architecture to production deployment.

CinéMood is my end-of-year portfolio project at Holberton School Bordeaux, where I'm studying application design and development. The full codebase is open source.
